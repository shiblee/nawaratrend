Common questions

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes. All orders above ₹999 ship free across India. A small delivery charge applies to orders below ₹999 and is shown at checkout.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive tracking details by SMS or email. You can also sign in and open My orders, or message us on WhatsApp with your order number.

How do returns work?

If something isn't right, contact us with your order number and a photo of the item. Our Returns & refunds policy explains the return window and how refunds are paid.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay with UPI, debit or credit cards, and Cash on Delivery where available. All online payments go through a secure payment gateway.
