Overview

In short: we ship across India, and orders above ₹999 ship free.

This Shipping Policy explains how and when we deliver your NawaraTrend order, what delivery costs, and what to do if something goes wrong on the way. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

Where we deliver

In short: to most PIN codes in India, through trusted courier partners.

We deliver to addresses within India only. We work with reputed courier partners to reach most PIN codes. If a courier does not serve your PIN code, we'll let you know before dispatch and offer a full refund or an alternative address.

We do not ship outside India at the moment.

Processing and delivery times

In short: we dispatch in 1–2 business days, and most orders arrive within 3–8 business days.

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays). Orders placed after 4 pm or on a Sunday are processed the next business day.

Cash on Delivery

In short: available on most PIN codes; please keep the exact amount ready.

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for most PIN codes. If it isn't available for yours, you'll see this at checkout.
  • Please keep the exact amount ready, as delivery partners may not carry change.
  • We may confirm COD orders by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Orders we cannot confirm may be cancelled.
  • Repeatedly refusing COD orders may lead to COD being turned off for your account.

Tracking your order

In short: you'll get a tracking link by SMS, email or WhatsApp once your order ships.

As soon as your order is dispatched, we'll send you the courier name and tracking number. You can also sign in and open My orders, or message us on WhatsApp with your order number for an update.

Receiving your parcel

In short: check the package at delivery, and tell us within 48 hours if anything is wrong.

  • Please check that the outer package is sealed and undamaged before accepting it. If it looks tampered with or badly damaged, you may refuse the delivery and contact us.
  • We recommend recording a short video while opening the parcel. It helps us resolve any issue quickly.
  • If an item is damaged, wrong or missing, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos or video. We'll arrange a replacement or refund as described in our Returns & Refunds Policy.

Failed or returned deliveries

In short: please give a correct address and phone number so the courier can reach you.

The courier will usually try to deliver up to three times. If delivery fails because the address is incomplete, nobody is available, or the parcel is refused, it will be returned to us.

For prepaid orders returned this way, we'll refund the product value after the parcel reaches us. Delivery charges, and any return shipping cost charged by the courier, may be deducted. You can also ask us to re-ship the order for an additional delivery charge.

Changing your address

In short: address changes are possible only before dispatch.

If you need to change your delivery address, contact us as soon as possible. We can update it before the order is dispatched. Once shipped, the address usually cannot be changed.
