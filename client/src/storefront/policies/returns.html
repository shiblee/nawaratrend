Overview

In short: if something isn't right, you can return most items within 7 days of delivery.

We want you to be happy with every order. This policy explains when you can cancel, return or exchange an item, and how refunds are paid. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

Cancelling an order

In short: free cancellation any time before dispatch.

  • You can cancel your order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. Contact us by phone or WhatsApp with your order number.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery or return the item under this policy instead.
  • For prepaid orders, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method.

Return window

In short: 7 days from delivery, in original condition.

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. To be accepted, the item must be:

  • unused, unwashed and in the same condition you received it;
  • in its original packaging, with all tags, labels, accessories, manuals and free gifts;
  • sent back with the invoice or order number.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

In short: tell us within 48 hours and we'll replace or refund it at no cost to you.

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos or an unboxing video. Once we've checked the details, we'll arrange a free pickup and send a replacement, or give you a full refund if a replacement isn't available.

Items that can't be returned

In short: for hygiene and safety reasons, some items can only be returned if they arrive damaged or wrong.

  • Opened beauty & skincare products
  • Innerwear & socks
  • Earbuds & in-ear products once opened
  • Items marked "non-returnable"
  • Gift cards

We also can't accept returns of items that are used, damaged by the customer, or missing their original packaging or tags.

How to return an item

In short: message us, we arrange a pickup, and we check the item when it arrives.

  • Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or the contact page with your order number, the item and the reason for return. Photos help.
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
  • Hand it to the courier. We arrange reverse pickup for most PIN codes. If pickup isn't available for yours, we'll ask you to send it by a courier of your choice and reimburse a reasonable shipping cost for approved returns.
  • Quality check. We inspect the item within 2 business days of receiving it and let you know the result.

Exchanges

In short: size or colour exchanges are free, subject to stock.

You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product, subject to availability. We ship the replacement once the original item passes the quality check. If the replacement isn't in stock, we'll refund you instead.
