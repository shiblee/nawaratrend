About this policy

In short: this explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

NawaraTrend ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect your personal data when you visit our website, contact us or place an order. It is written in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described here. This policy forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

What we collect

In short: only what we need to sell, deliver and support your orders.

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address.
  • Order details: products bought, order value, payment method and order history.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form, phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, IP address and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools.
  • Account activity: when you sign in and out of your account, and which products you look at while signed in, so we can keep your account secure and understand what our customers are interested in. Product visits aren't recorded if you turn off analytics cookies.

We do not collect or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Payments are handled directly by our payment gateway.

How we use your data

In short: to process orders, deliver them, support you and improve our store.

  • to confirm, process, ship and deliver your orders;
  • to handle returns, refunds and customer support;
  • to send order updates by SMS, email or WhatsApp;
  • to prevent fraud and misuse, including Cash on Delivery abuse;
  • to understand how the website is used and improve it;
  • to send offers and news, only if you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

In short: only with partners who help us run the store. We never sell your data.

  • Courier partners, who receive your name, phone number and address to deliver your order.
  • Payment gateways, who process online payments securely.
  • Service providers for website hosting, messaging and analytics, who act only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, where we are required to by law or to protect our rights and customers.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

Cookies

In short: necessary cookies keep the store working; analytics and marketing cookies are used only if you agree.

We use cookies and similar browser storage in three groups:

  • Necessary: your cart, wishlist, sign-in, security, and remembering your cookie choice. These are always on because the store can't work without them.
  • Analytics: anonymous statistics (Google Analytics) about which pages are visited, so we can improve the store. Used only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Marketing: measuring our ads and showing more relevant offers on other sites. Used only if you accept marketing cookies.

When you first visit, we ask for your choice. You can change it any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. We ask again every 6 months. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser, but parts of the store, such as the cart, may not work without them.

How we protect it

In short: secure connections, limited access and trusted partners.

Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to customer data is limited to staff and partners who need it to serve you. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no online service can be completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

How long we keep it

In short: only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires.

We keep order and invoice records for as long as required under tax and accounting laws. Other personal data is kept only while your account is active or while we need it for the purposes above. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

In short: you can see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update your data;
  • ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw consent, for example to marketing messages;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf;
  • raise a complaint with our grievance officer and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy

In short: our store is for adults.

Our website is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us their data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

In short: if we update this policy, we'll publish the new version on this page.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always the one published on this page. If we make important changes, we'll highlight them on the website.
